Setting up a session: six options

An audit of what the generation flow actually gates, then six ways to replace the three-card modality question — plus what to do about the other choose-one widgets in the thread. Every widget below is drawn at 348px, the real width of the chat pane.

What the audit found

!
Sound effects are not a layer you can offer.
Two places strip it on purpose. agent.py: "SFX isn't a shipped layer yet, so it is intentionally NOT planned." tools/impls.py filters layer != AUDIO_LAYER_SFX out of every plan, even if the model asks for it. A checklist with SFX checked by default would be the console promising a track the pipeline silently drops.
✓
A checklist is a faithful shape for the other two.
The gate maps to (mode, layers): full_audio → full_e2e,[music,voiceover]; music_only → music_first,[music]; voiceover_only → music_first,[voiceover]. The mode is derivable from the layer count, so two checkboxes carry exactly the same information as three exclusive cards — nothing is lost.
i
This gate is routing, not spending.
Money is gated separately and later, by confirmSpend() on Use this and on Generate voice-over. So checking both layers by default costs nothing — it just means the agent also drafts a script. The fear that a permissive default burns credits doesn't apply here.
!
The three cards were built for a full-width thread.
.cards-row uses flex:1 1 150px. That was fine in a 680px centred column; in the 380px pane it wraps 2 + 1 and the third card sits alone on its own line. This is most of why the widget looks wrong now — it isn't only a content problem.
i
One setup question is already being asked in the wrong place.
"Keep the video's original audio under the music" lives in the mix panel, three steps later. It's a fact about the footage you already have, not a balance decision — and the observation pane knows the answer (detected_include_vocals) before you're asked. Options 3 and 5 pull it forward.
i
Two proposal fields are in the model and never rendered.
MusicProposalCard.include_vocals and vocal_gender exist on every direction and appear nowhere in the UI. Instrumental-vs-vocal is the single most audible difference between two directions, and it's free — see section B.

Two notes from the table. Takes stacks three full-width buttons under each card — Use this, New variation, Download track — which reads as three equal actions when only the first one advances the flow; the other two belong behind an overflow. And the mix panel grows from one slider to four the moment a voice-over exists, which is the largest single jump in visual weight anywhere in the thread.

What I'd actually do

  1. Option 2, with Option 1 behind "Change." The recommendation is the fast path and the checklist is the escape hatch — you get the one-click case without losing the explicit control, and neither has to serve both jobs badly.
  2. Show SFX exactly once, disabled. Wherever the checklist appears. It's the cheapest possible answer to a question every new user will have, and it stops anyone re-adding it to the plan by mistake.
  3. Keep the gate blocking. The audit is clear that it's routing, not spend, and that the whole point is a modality decision that survives independent of the LLM. Option 6 is tempting and I'd leave it on the shelf.
  4. Ship B1 this week. Two fields you already store, rendered. It's the highest ratio of visible improvement to work in this whole document.
  5. Scope B3 separately. The energy arc is the real answer to "more visual, less text" — but it needs a field in MusicProposalCard that generation honours, and drawing it before that guarantee exists would break the rule the timeline is built on.
