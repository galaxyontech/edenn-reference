How Edenn works

1 · Start. Upload a video, say what you want ("cinematic and premium"), and start the session. The director watches your footage first, then brings back directions.

2 · Direct. Pick a direction and approve generation. Ask for anything in plain words — new takes, a voiceover, sound effects, a different mood. Sliders fine-tune the mix.

3 · Timeline & Canvas. The right pane shows your video with its audio laid out on the same clock — music, voice-over, and effects against the scene cuts. Switch to Canvas to see every take as a branching tree: play, compare, branch, and comment right on the nodes.

4 · Collaborate. Share mints a link with a role (view / comment / iterate). Collaborators join under their own name; mention @Edenn Director in a thread to hand work to the agent.

5 · Finish. Lock the take you love; Export downloads the final mix on your video.
